SUNAT electronic invoicing for TypeScript.

- [Tribu](https://github.com/tribu-pe/tribu) - Issue invoices, receipts and notes to Peru's tax authority. ([En español](https://github.com/tribu-pe/tribu/blob/main/README.es.md))
- [@tribu-pe/pdf](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@tribu-pe/pdf) - Printed representation of SUNAT documents issued with Tribu: A4 and ticket templates on pdfmake.

Brand assets: [tribu-pe/brand](https://github.com/tribu-pe/brand) - logo, palette, icons and guidelines.
